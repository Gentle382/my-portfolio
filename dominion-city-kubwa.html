01

Overview

One home for church life: services, communities, media, giving and account.

Dominion City Kubwa wanted a digital space that serves long-time members and first-time visitors alike. I designed the mobile app end to end, from research to a clickable prototype handed over to developers.

Users
Members, first-time visitors, church workers
Core features
Events, communities, giving, video messages, account
My scope
Research, IA, flows, UI, prototype
02

Problem

Members and visitors couldn’t easily find what was happening, where to belong, or how to give — without asking someone.

Information is scattered

Announcements lived across chat groups, social posts and the pulpit.

Belonging is unclear

Visitors didn’t know which community or unit to join, or who to ask.

Giving has friction

Giving outside service meant copying bank details by hand.

How might we…bring announcements, community, media and giving into one place that feels welcoming to a first-time visitor and fast for a regular member?

04

Insights

What we learned — and what I designed in response.

  1. 01

    Sunday isn’t the only touchpoint

    Members want to know what’s on during the week, not just at service.

    Design responseAn events-first home showing this week’s gatherings.
  2. 02

    Visitors need a guided path

    First-timers felt unsure how to connect after their first visit.

    Design responseOnboarding that ends with a clear “Find a community” step.
  3. 03

    Messages get rewatched

    People revisit sermons to take notes and share them.

    Design responseA searchable video library with speaker and date.
  4. 04

    Giving must feel safe and quick

    Trust and speed mattered more than extra features.

    Design responseTwo-step giving with clear confirmation and history.
05

Persona

Designing for Tobi.

Tobi, 26
Young professional · new to the church

“I enjoyed the service, but I didn’t know how to get more involved.”

Goals
  • Find events and groups that fit his week
  • Rewatch messages on his commute
  • Give without hunting for account details
Frustrations
  • Missing announcements made during service
  • Not knowing who to ask about joining a unit
  • Copying bank details for every offering
Needs
  • A welcoming first-run experience
  • A clear calendar of gatherings
  • Fast, trustworthy giving

12

Lessons learned

What I’d carry into the next project.

  • Design for two audiences at onceVisitors and members needed different starting points from the same home screen.
  • Content strategy is designWithout fresh announcements and media, the best layout feels empty — so I designed slots staff can update easily.
  • Test where people actually areShort tests with members after service caught issues no design review would have.
