A product designer who speaks fluent engineering.

I’m Chibueze Wisdom Ugochukwu, a UI/UX and Product Designer and Full-Stack Developer with a background in engineering and technology. I enjoy transforming complex problems into simple, intuitive digital experiences.

My approach combines user-centred design, visual design, product thinking and hands-on full-stack development — so what I design is not only usable, but buildable, and I can build it.
